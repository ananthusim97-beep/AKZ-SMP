About the SMP

Ashvale is a survival multiplayer world built by its players, not for them. There is no scripted questline and no admin-written plot — only a shared map, a set of rules, and whatever the people who log in decide to make of it. Some come to build. Some come to trade. Some come to start wars they can't finish. Every alliance, betrayal, empire, and ruin on this map exists because someone chose to make it happen.

This page is a living record of that world — who's playing, what's been built, and the moments worth remembering. Replace this placeholder copy with your own server's story once things get moving.

The World

A single continuous overworld seed, played on hard survival with a handful of community-voted rules layered on top. No resets planned — what gets built, stays built.

Rules

  1. No griefing outside declared wars. If it isn't your build and there's no active conflict, leave it alone.
  2. Declare wars before you start them. Post in Discord so it's official — and so everyone knows the story is happening.
  3. No cheating or exploiting bugs. Duplication glitches, X-ray, and similar are an instant ban.
  4. Keep spawn and shared builds neutral ground. No PvP or destruction within the spawn radius.
  5. Be excellent to each other outside the game, too. In-character rivalry stays in-character.
